How it works

Every enquiry becomes an opportunity

Intelligent Broker connects every enquiry to inventory – whether it arrives on WhatsApp, through the website or as a recorded phone call.

1📞

Customer enquiry

WhatsApp message, website call-back request, or a recorded call uploaded with the customer's number.

2🎙️

Speech-to-text & AI

Dictate or paste the transcript. We extract budget, location, type, model and preferences.

3🗂️

Lead board

Every requirement becomes a lead you can move across New → Matched → Contacted → Closed.

4✅

Matching engine

Match: share details in one tap. No match: lead waits and is auto-matched the moment you add suitable stock.

Questions

Do customers need an account?

No. Anyone can browse listings and request a call back with just a name and phone number.

Who can see my leads?

Only you. Leads and customers are tied to your broker account and are never shown to other brokers.

How does speech-to-text work here?

In Chrome and Edge you can dictate using the microphone button. In production, a speech-to-text API would transcribe uploaded recordings automatically.

Where is my data stored?

This demo keeps everything in your own browser. Use Settings → Export to back it up, or Import to restore it.
